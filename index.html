🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

What information does StudentHub store?

StudentHub may store information you enter into the app, such as your name, class schedule, study preferences, expenses, and notification settings.

Where is your information stored?

In the current version of StudentHub, your information is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage.

This means StudentHub does not currently send this information to a StudentHub server.

Course materials and external websites

StudentHub may provide links to external websites and learning resources. When you open one of these links, you are leaving StudentHub and becoming subject to that website's own privacy policy and terms.

Notifications

If you enable class reminders, StudentHub uses your browser's notification system to remind you about upcoming classes. You can disable reminders at any time.

Your control

You control the information you enter into StudentHub. You can delete saved classes and expenses from within the app.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as StudentHub develops new features or changes how information is handled.

❓ Help & FAQ

Quick answers to common StudentHub questions.

📅 How do I add a class?

Go to My Classes and tap + Add Class. Enter your course, day, time, and room, then save it.

📚 How does the Study Planner work?

StudentHub uses your saved class schedule and study preferences to suggest suitable times for studying.

🔔 How do class reminders work?

Enable reminders and choose how many minutes before class you want to be notified. Your browser will then provide the reminder.

📴 Can I use StudentHub offline?

StudentHub is designed to work offline for important features that have already been loaded and saved on your device.

💰 Where are my expenses stored?

Your expenses are currently stored locally in your browser on your device.

📖 Where do the course materials come from?

StudentHub provides links to selected learning resources and external websites. Some resources are provided by third-party services.

📱 Can I install StudentHub?

Yes. When your browser supports installation, tap the Install App button to install StudentHub as a Progressive Web App.

📩 How can I contact StudentHub?

Visit the Contact section to send us an email or connect with StudentHub on Instagram.

📚 Study Guides

Simple ways to study smarter.

📝 How to Study for Exams Prepare smarter, not harder. +

Study with a plan.

Don't try to learn everything at once. Break your course into smaller topics and give each one a clear target.

After studying, close your notes and test yourself. Then practice with past questions and spend extra time on topics you find difficult.

🎯 Try this: Pick one topic, study for 30 minutes, close your notes, and write down what you remember.
🧠 Active Recall Learn by testing your memory. +

Don't just read. Recall.

Read a topic, close your notes, and try to explain what you remember. Then check your notes and see what you missed.

You can use questions, flashcards, brain dumps, or even teach the topic to someone else.

⚡ Remember: Read → Close → Recall → Check → Repeat.
📅 Build a Study Routine Make studying a habit. +

Consistency beats perfection.

Choose a realistic study time and start with manageable sessions. Your routine should fit around your classes and everyday life.

Give every session a specific goal and take short breaks. If you miss a day, don't give up — simply start again.

🎯 Try this: Choose one study time and use it consistently for the next 7 days.
